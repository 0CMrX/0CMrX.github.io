I am a Ph.D. student in [School of Cybersecurity and Privacy](https://scp.cc.gatech.edu/) at Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by [Prof. Vassilis Zikas](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~vzikas/) and [Prof. Vladimir Kolesnikov](https://faculty.cc.gatech.edu/~vlad/).

Previously, I obtained my bachelor's degree from the [Institute for Interdisciplinary Information Sciences](https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/) (Yao Class) at Tsinghua University, advised by [Prof. Zhixuan Fang](https://people.iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/~fang/). I was very fortunate to work as a visiting researcher with [Prof. Fan Zhang](https://www.fanzhang.me/) in Yale University, and [Prof. Jiaheng Zhang](https://zjhzjh123.github.io/) in National University of Singapore (NUS).

My research interests lie in applied cryptography. I'm also particularly interested in blockchain technologies and distributed computing.
